vijay

C. Joseph Vijay, widely known as Thalapathy Vijay, is the 9th Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu, having assumed office on May 10, 2026. A former leading actor in Tamil cinema and founder of the Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK) political party, he transitioned from a three-decade acting career to politics, winning the 2026 state elections as part of a coalition government.
